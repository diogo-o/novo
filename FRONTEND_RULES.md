# Frontend Rules

These are the confirmed Beta frontend constraints. Do not add generic design-system requirements or
invent interaction behavior beyond them.

## Visual Frame

- Target desktop composition: **1366x768**.
- Preserve the established old/current visual language where applicable.
- Keep headers and tabs consistent across pages.
- The top operational structure is **Planeamento / Controlo / Boquilhas**.
- Planeamento exposes Job On.
- Controlo exposes its internal areas according to the user profile.
- Side panels follow the established Beta layout and remain below the relevant tab structure.

## Tables and Selection

- Do not place action buttons inside tables.
- A single click selects a row.
- Action buttons for the selected row sit outside the table.
- A double click opens the selected item or detail.

## Information Preservation

- Never remove important functional information merely to simplify a page.
- Preserve Tool reference, lot, and context information.
- Preserve OK/NOK information.
- Preserve MCaliper links wherever they are defined.
- Avoid duplicated context blocks; show the relevant production context once in the established
  location.
